Team Sparrowhawk

The Write-Up Assistant Solution

An AI‑powered tool that generates draft assessment reports from healthcare professional (HCP) inputs, such as assessment notes and selected descriptors, for review and finalisation by the HCP.

  • Helps reduce the time HCPs spend writing reports from scratch.
  • Produces a consistent draft structure using the information entered by the HCP.
  • Supports, but does not replace, professional judgement, keeping the human in the loop.
